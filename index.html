02 — Process

How an incident moves

01

Triage

Assess impact and urgency, confirm scope, and decide whether this is a fault, a misconfiguration or something that warrants escalation.

02

Investigate

Gather technical evidence — logs, firewall and filtering policy, authentication records, switch port state — and work back to root cause.

03

Escalate

Route to the right internal team or vendor with the findings attached, so the next person starts from evidence rather than from scratch.

04

Document

Record the resolution in ServiceNow and Confluence clearly enough that the same issue is faster to resolve the second time.
